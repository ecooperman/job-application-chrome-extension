Job Application Autofill

This is your template - what gets copied into application fields. Everything is stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local), never sent anywhere except the optional AI drafting call to Anthropic below.

Personal info

Work authorization

Answered with a word or short phrase - matching is fuzzy, so "Yes" or "No" works fine.

Voluntary self-identification (EEO)

Optional. Leave blank to skip these fields on forms rather than guessing.

Preferences

Work history

Used to give the AI fallback real context when it drafts answers to custom questions.

Education

Snippet bank

Pre-written answers for common custom questions (e.g. "why this company", "greatest strength", "tell us about a challenge"). If a question's text contains most of a snippet's keywords, that answer is used instead of calling the AI.

AI fallback

When a custom question doesn't match any snippet above, optionally have Claude draft an answer from your work history and education (still inserted for you to review, never auto-submitted). Get a key at console.anthropic.com/settings/keys.
